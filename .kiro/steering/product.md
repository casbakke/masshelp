# Product

## Mission

No one who meets the rule should lose their health insurance over paperwork. MassHelp helps MassHealth members prove what is already true, on time, in a form MassHealth will accept, so they keep their coverage.

## Who it serves

MassHealth CarePlus members (expansion adults aged 19 to 64) who must meet the new work and community engagement rule starting January 1, 2027. Most already meet it. The risk is failing the paperwork, not the rule. Most at risk:

- Gig workers, whose app income does not show up in state wage data
- Students, whose enrollment may not be shared automatically
- People with disabilities, who often must document medical frailty
- People with limited English
- People without reliable internet or stable housing

## Principles

- Assume the person qualifies.
- Plain language first. Every message should make sense to someone reading it on a phone, in a hurry, in their second language.
- Meet people where they are. A phone call or a text, no app download. English and Spanish end to end.
- Accessible by default. Usable by people with disabilities.
- Privacy is non-negotiable. Collect the minimum, explain why, keep it secure, and never sell or share it without consent.
- Neutral and accurate. Match current MassHealth rules. When unsure, say so and point to an official source or a human.
- Augment, do not replace. Support MassHealth, navigators, and community organizations.

## What MassHelp is not

- Not an advocacy campaign for or against work requirements
- Not a substitute for MassHealth's official determination
- Not a legal service, though it connects people to legal aid
- Not a data-collection product

## MVP non-goals

- No outbound contact. The member always starts. The agent never calls or texts first, except a confirmation the member was told about.
- No real MassHealth integration, real member data, or real submission. All MassHealth systems are mocks.
- No UI. No web app, navigator console, or admin dashboard. SMS (Twilio) and voice (Vapi) only.
- No eligibility decisions. Always say "likely" and "MassHealth makes the final decision."
- No legal advice and no appeals handling. Give the MassHealth number (1-800-841-2900) and suggest legal aid.
- Never collect SSN, bank account, or card numbers.
